# v0.15 — verified move animations and native Kashimo visuals
Forge 1.20.1 · JujutsuCraft ver51 · Easy NPC 7.15.0 · CustomNPCs GBPort

## Cause of unrelated raised-arm poses
Previously AbilityCatalog.animationFor() guessed that almost every
unknown procedure should play `right_arm_front`, `both_arm_front`,
or `both_arm_up`. The original JJK files for those poses contain
almost no movement (some are one constant keyframe). This was responsible
for NPCs raising their arms when the move wasn't a gesture at all.

**Fix:** There is now NO universal fallback. A curated subset of actual
animation files is selected for physically corresponding melee attacks,
combos, kicks, weapon swings, known hand gestures, and domains.
Unmapped moves run without an artificial pose rather than using an
unrelated animation. All mapped animation resource names were checked
against the installed v51 original JAR.

## Duplicate application through inheritance
HumanoidModel.setupAnim is called from PlayerModel.setupAnim, which
is also called from CustomNPCs ModelClassicPlayer.setupAnim. The older
implementation could apply the same JJK pose two or three times during
one render pass, further freezing/extending rotations.

**Fix:** HumanoidModel hook skips PlayerModel subclasses. PlayerModel
hook delegates CustomNPCs models to the CustomNPCs-specific TAIL hook
and EasyNPC entities to their model-manager setupAnimationEnd hook.
This retains one final pose-applier for each targeted humanoid model.

## Animation timing
Original keyframe intervals are now played closer to their original
combat cadence. Previous v0.13 playback stretched a 0.2s punch sequence
out to about 0.6s and held the last pose too long. Current playback:
* Final native keyframe generally reached at 83% of the display window;
* animation window ~1.12 × native motion + 0.18s, bounded to 0.42–1.65s;
* quick 55ms fade-in and 110ms fade-out.
* Original held-charge behavior remains sustained until release.

This aims for sharper, more dynamic hits instead of prolonged held poses.

## Kashimo's unusual floating particles
v0.12 invented generic vanilla `ELECTRIC_SPARK` and `END_ROD` particles
and played a vanilla lightning bolt impact sound when an NPC used Lightning
Strike. The original Kashimo/JJK procedure in v51 uses proprietary blue
thunder particles (`particle_thunder_blue` and mini version) plus
`electric_shock` / `thunder_impact` sounds.

**Fix:** Removed all added vanilla END_ROD / ELECTRIC_SPARK particles.
When Kashimo uses his target-locked Lightning Strike, the addon now
spawns only the real JujutsuCraft `particle_thunder_blue` at the locked
enemy and plays its native `jujutsucraft:thunder_impact` sound. It
does NOT stack passive charge marks or target nearby bystanders.
Move's individual damage and cooldown settings still apply, and vanilla
damage protection remains in place.

## Preserved v0.14 functionality
Server-wide player/NPC hit-sound toggles, target-locked Kashimo lightning,
per-move damage, domain sure-hit overrides, charge/crouch modes, the
v0.6 NPC-versus-NPC melee compatibility bridge, original domain settings,
conditional weapon right-click moves, backdash, RCT, grey Kashimo hair,
and editor navigation.

## In-game validation
1. Remove older Jujutsu NPC Compat JARs and use a backed-up test world.
2. Run `/jjknpc version` — should say **v0.15**.
3. Run `/jjknpc animate <npc> punch_right`; verify upper-body rotation,
   arm and leg motion without a stuck standing pose.
4. Fight with Kashimo and compare with v0.14: native blue thunder should
   appear on the current target; rising white END_ROD particles must not.
5. Try a mix of scripted moves and combat/weapon moves on Easy NPC and
   CustomNPCs humanoid renderers. Unmapped moves should NOT automatically
   play arm-up/front poses.
6. Verify server sound toggles still work.

Forge compilation/test/packaging is NOT an in-game animation validation.
If a specific move still looks wrong, provide its exact name and a clip.
The native animation skeleton still has bones absent from vanilla
HumanoidModel (e.g. dedicated torso, hips, armor bones); those cannot
always be reproduced exactly on the six-bone rig.
