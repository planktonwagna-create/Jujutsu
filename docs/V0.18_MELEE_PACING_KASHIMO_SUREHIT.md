# v0.18 — Melee windup/duel pacing, sparse Kashimo sure-hit lightning

Forge 1.20.1 / JujutsuCraft v51 / Easy NPC 7.15 / CustomNPCs GBPort / optional JCraft 0.18.2.

## 1. Ordinary melee attempt animation, not hit-confirmation animation

Before v0.18, the JCraft combat animations fired in `LivingDamageEvent`,
so NPCs that missed, were blocked, or had attacks canceled had no animation.
It could also show an animation after the hit had already landed.

v0.18 registers `LivingAttackEvent` on the Forge server to intercept direct
ordinary attacks by configured NPCs. Rather than allowing that native melee
damage to resolve before the animation, the addon queues a **6 tick animation
windup (0.3 sec)**, plays the appropriate JCraft Brawler/Anubis full-body
animation right away, then performs the original NPC's
`Mob.doHurtTarget(target)` once at the intended impact frame.

**The pose always plays even if the target subsequently dodges or blocks.**
Damage is never guaranteed for regular melee; it is only attempted if the
target is still in reach and sight at impact. Other NPC mods' damage and
faction rules remain in effect. The old v0.6 NPC combat fallback is retained
only for explicit opt-in configured NPC targets. No animation is triggered
from successful `LivingDamageEvent` any more.

## 2. 1–2 second server-configurable melee buffer

Setting in `world/serverconfig/jujutsunpccompat-server.toml`:

```toml
[combat]
    meleeAttackBufferTicks = 30
```

20 ticks = 1 second; 30 ticks = **1.5 seconds default**;
40 ticks = 2 seconds. Applies to ordinary NPC melee, not cursed technique
specific cooldowns or a player's own attacks.

The same lock also staggers two compatible NPCs fighting each other,
so they cannot both begin an ordinary melee strike simultaneously. It
does not create a global cooldown for unrelated fights occurring elsewhere.

## 3. Kashimo: direct locked target, original JJK curse damage

The addon previously used `DamageSources.mobAttack` for Kashimo's
targeted lightning. The original JujutsuCraft v51 RangeAttack procedure
uses the mod's registered `jujutsucraft:damage_curse` type.

v0.18 uses `caster.damageSources().source(...damage_curse, caster)`,
still directly targeting the enemy selected at cast start. Lightning is
**not a raycast**: the zigzag does not need to collide with a hitbox, and
a moving/dodging target stays locked. The spell doesn't select another
victim or damage bystanders. It no longer stops solely because the
target moves outside the initial 128-block range after casting.
As with JujutsuCraft's original `hurt` damage path, creative/invulnerable
entities, Forge cancellation hooks and normal Minecraft damage rules can
still block damage. No workaround secretly edits health.

If the particle registry cannot be loaded, the visual is omitted but
the scheduled damage still resolves at impact. Target death/unload or
switching dimensions can still cancel the attack legitimately.

## 4. Lightning visual

The bolt uses only JujutsuCraft's own blue and blue-mini thunder particles.
Compared with v0.17 it now uses:
* 2-axis, deterministic, irregular *piecewise zigzag* bends (12 nodes);
* reduced per-segment particles (2–10 samples rather than 4–18);
* a smaller blue-thunder impact burst (3+6 instead of 9+16);
* a small single-particle residual tail instead of four;
* ongoing homing onto the original living target.

## 5. Preserved systems

All v0.17 JCraft Brawler/Anubis/dash animation integration,
JJK technique animations, Kashimo configurable damage and cooldown,
server-wide player/NPC hit-sound settings, GUI, domains,
RCT, shield/anti-domain options, weapon moves,
NPC melee damage-compat, and grey Kashimo hair are preserved.

## In-game test

1. Back up the world; replace v0.17 with v0.18 JAR.
2. Verify `/jjknpc version` is v0.18.
3. With two configured NPCs in a duel, enable combat-damage compatibility
   on the intended NPC target. Observe that they alternate ordinary
   melee attempts rather than swinging simultaneously.
4. Have a victim dodge at a swing: the attack should animate anyway,
   but deal no damage if the victim is out of reach.
5. Try both sword-equipped and unarmed NPCs, with JCraft installed.
6. Test Kashimo against a moving NPC. The blue bolt should be less dense,
   irregular/zigzag, remain locked and hit the selected opponent via the
   native JJK `damage_curse` source.
7. Verify old domains, RCT and the server-wide sound toggles still work.

A successful Forge compilation does NOT validate actual multiplayer
visuals, combat timing with all custom NPC forks, or JJK mod interactions.
